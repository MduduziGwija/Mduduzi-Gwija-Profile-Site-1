01 About me

From public administration to building the systems it runs on.

I'm Mduduzi, based in Cape Town. I hold an Honours degree in Public Administration and work in the public sector as an Institutional Coordination Officer in a bursary management unit, supporting university bursary students. It's a public administration role, but I also design and develop SharePoint systems for my organisation, and IT is where I want to build my career.

I started as an intern in operational support and financial administration, and kept noticing the same thing: good people losing hours to spreadsheets, paper forms and email chains. So I started building. Power Automate flows, a Power Apps lease register, SharePoint restructuring, then full web apps backed by real databases.

Today I have shipped production tools at work and independent apps on my own, and I run Novela Solutions Africa on the side. Now I want to branch into IT professionally, keep expanding my experience and learn alongside people who build for a living. If your team values curiosity and follow-through, let's talk.
